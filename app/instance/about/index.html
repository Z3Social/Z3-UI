About Z3 Social

Z3 Social is an independent, sovereign social platform designed to give individuals and communities complete freedom of expression, ownership over their identity, and a modern, high-performance social networking experience.

Site Rules & Terms

By signing up for Z3 Social, you agree not to:

  1. Use our service to send spam or scam users.
  2. Promote violence, terrorism, or severe physical harm on publicly viewable feeds, groups, or channels.
  3. Post illegal content recognized as unlawful across jurisdictions (including Child Sexual Abuse Material / CSAM, human trafficking, or selling illegal firearms and illicit drugs).
  4. Intentionally create multiple accounts to harass or circumvent user blocks.
  5. Post sexually explicit or adult (NSFW) material without marking it as sensitive, or without enabling appropriate content warning tags.
  6. Hate speech or propaganda inciting violence or discrimination. While cursing, profanity, adult humor, and lewd jokes are fully allowed and protected as free expression, any advocacy of national, racial, or religious hatred that constitutes incitement to lawless violence or to any other similar illegal action against any person or group of persons on any grounds including race, color, religion, language, or national origin (in reference to Article 13, Subsection 5 of the American Convention on Human Rights / Pact of San José, Costa Rica: “toda apología del odio nacional, racial o religioso que constituyan incitaciones a la violencia o cualquier otra acción ilegal similar contra cualquier persona o grupo de personas, por ningún motivo, inclusive los de raza, color, religión, idioma u origen nacional”) is strictly prohibited.

Strict Age Requirement (18+)

Z3 Social is strictly for adults (18 years of age or older). This platform is not intended for children, minors, or teenagers under 18 (or 17+ adult maturity where legally restricted). Anyone under the age of 18 is prohibited from registering or creating an account on Z3 Social. Age assurance and verification signals are strictly enforced.

Free Speech & Open Discourse

Z3 Social is built to maximize freedom of speech and expression. What will not get you banned or censored:

  • Hurting someone's feelings or speaking hard truths.
  • Unpopular, controversial, political, or dissenting viewpoints voiced freely.
  • Disagreements and interpersonal disputes that can be handled through personal blocking or muting.
  • Cursing, swearing, adult humor, and lewd jokes (these are 100% permitted and will never get you banned, provided they do not cross into racist hate speech or advocacy of hatred inciting violence or discrimination under Article 13(5) of the Pact of San José).
  • Consensual adult (NSFW) and sensitive creative media, provided it complies with our content warning and profile blur standards.

Adult & NSFW Content Policy

Adult and sensitive media (NSFW) is permitted on Z3 Social subject to responsible labeling and protection for sensitive viewers:

  • Content Warning & Blurs: Any post containing nudity, sexual content, or graphic material must be marked as sensitive. Media is blurred by default with explicit content warnings across timelines and feeds.
  • Profile Blurring & Warnings: Profiles primarily sharing NSFW content can be flagged or self-labeled, enabling automatic profile blurs and warnings before other users view media and timelines.
  • Age Assurance & UK OSA Compliance: In accordance with the UK Online Safety Act and age assurance standards, access to adult profiles, unblurred media, and sensitive material is restricted exclusively to verified adult users (18+).

Platform & Technology

Z3 Social is powered by the sovereign Z3-Engine backend and the reactive Z3-UI client, utilizing open AT Protocol-derived architectures and modern web standards.
